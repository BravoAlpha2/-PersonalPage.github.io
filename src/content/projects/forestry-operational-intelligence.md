## Before → after

**Before.** A request arrives → someone draws the parcel by hand → a spreadsheet is updated → a crew is phoned → nobody knows the status until they come back.

**After.** Request logged in Monday.com → parcel pulled from the GIS layer → ODT generated from a template → approval and crew allocation tracked → dashboard shows every open job, its location and its status.
